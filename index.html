Who Was Looting
Los Angeles During the Fires?

In January 2025 the Palisades and Eaton fires became the most destructive in Los Angeles history: the Palisades fire on the coastal Westside, and the Eaton fire, which burned Altadena. The LA County District Attorney, Santa Monica Police, and California Highway Patrol publicly named 32 looting suspects. All 32 are below, with two non theft arrests from the same sweep shown separately, and every racial coding is verified to a jail booking record or a published law enforcement photograph.

Method

Scope. Every suspect publicly named in the looting crackdown for the January 2025 Palisades and Eaton fires: 13 named by the District Attorney, 18 by Santa Monica PD, 1 by CHP, plus two non theft arrests from the same SMPD releases, shown separately and excluded from every chart. The agencies chose who to name; this page includes all of them and omits none. It is a floor, not a census: LASD reported 67 looting or burglary arrests in the Eaton zone alone and SMPD over 40 in its evacuation zone, most never named. The DA fire crime total of 27 also includes 14 arson, impersonation, and disaster entry defendants outside this ledger, listed in the county February 6 roundup.

Coding. Race comes only from official booking records or published law enforcement photographs, reviewed by human eyes, never from names: 14 from LASD booking records pulled August 10, 2026, with the record screenshots attached to each card, and 20 from agency published photographs. Every name estimate used during verification matched the record when it landed, and none was charted before it did. Hispanic is a standalone category.

Presumption of innocence. Everyone here is charged or arrested, not convicted. Both Altadena cases were still pretrial as of June 2026; Mastey was booked and released, no filing confirmed.
